DIVISION 02 • LIFE SAFETY & FIRE COMPLIANCE

Intelligent Addressable Fire Alarm Systems

NBC 2016-compliant addressable fire detection, optical heat and smoke sensing, gas suppression integration, and rapid automated evacuation sequencing.

Life Safety Automation & NBC 2016 Compliance

Digitech designs, installs, tests, and certifies intelligent addressable fire detection systems tailored for commercial buildings, factories, schools, and hospitals. Our life safety architectures conform to National Building Code (NBC 2016) and NFPA 72 regulations to ensure immediate life protection and safe evacuation.

Key Engineering Features

  • Addressable Optical Smoke & Thermal Detectors: Microprocessor-based dual-sensor heads minimize false alarms while providing pinpoint hazard location.
  • Building Automation & Damper Interlocks: Automatic HVAC damper shutdown, elevator grounding, access door release, and fire pump status monitoring.
  • Clean Agent Gas Suppression (FM-200 / Novec): Specialized fire suppression systems designed for data centers, UPS rooms, and electrical control rooms.
  • Fire NOC Audit & Annual Compliance Testing: Comprehensive quarterly inspection, sensor recalibration, and statutory NOC paperwork support.
