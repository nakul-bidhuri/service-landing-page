About Digitech Services

Over two decades of engineering excellence in mission-critical electronic surveillance, life safety automation, and automated health monitoring across India.

ENGINEERING HERITAGE

Trusted Partner for Zero-Downtime Facility Security

Founded in 2004 in New Delhi, Digitech Services has grown from a specialized security systems contractor into one of India's premier providers of turnkey electronic security and surveillance infrastructure.

With over 50,000 deployments across commercial complexes, industrial plants, government facilities, and healthcare campuses, we combine rigorous optical engineering, NBC-compliant fire safety, and our proprietary Digitech HMS (Health Monitoring System) to ensure that critical surveillance never fails silently.

Core Engineering Principles
