DIVISION 04 • ACOUSTIC COMMUNICATIONS & VOICE EVACUATION

Intelligent Public Address & Voice Alarm (PA/VA) Systems

EN54-16 compliant network audio streaming, multi-zone acoustic paging, emergency voice evacuation sequencing, and automated life safety broadcasts.

Acoustic Intelligibility & Phased Emergency Evacuation

Digitech designs, installs, and tunes mission-critical Public Address and Voice Alarm (PA/VA) architectures. In an emergency, pre-recorded voice instructions prevent panic far more effectively than traditional bells or sirens. Our network-driven systems guarantee clear acoustic intelligibility across corporate offices, industrial shop floors, auditoriums, and transport hubs.

Key Engineering Features

  • EN54-16 Certified Voice Evacuation Systems: High-intelligibility phased evacuation messaging automatically triggered by fire alarm panel contact closures or IP triggers.
  • Multi-Zone IP-Based Digital Audio Paging: Dante / AES67 network-controlled audio matrix with custom zoning for floor-by-floor or selective building broadcasts.
  • Acoustic Simulation & STI Intelligibility Tuning: Precision speaker placement (ceiling, column, horn) calculated via EASE acoustic modeling to eliminate echo and blindspots.
  • Background Music (BGM) & Ambient Noise Sensing: Dynamic volume adjustment sensing ambient crowd noise for retail spaces, hospitals, and high-footfall atriums.
