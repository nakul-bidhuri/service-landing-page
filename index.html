Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about installations, AMC policies, warranties, and Digitech HMS integration.

What exactly is Digitech HMS and how does it prevent security failures?

Digitech HMS (Hardware Health Monitoring System) is our automated telemetry platform that continuously polls your cameras, NVRs, and storage drives 24/7. It instantly detects video signal loss, camera angle tampering, failing hard drive sectors, and recording dropouts. Instead of discovering an issue after an incident has occurred, HMS notifies our engineers immediately so repairs can be executed proactively.

Can Digitech take over and service our existing CCTV / Fire Alarm setup under AMC?

Yes, absolutely. We offer both Non-Comprehensive and Comprehensive AMCs for legacy and third-party systems. Our senior engineer performs a thorough health audit of your cabling, power supplies, camera alignment, and NVR settings before onboarding you to our guaranteed 4-hour SLA support network.

How fast is the turnaround time for on-site breakdown support?

For priority clients and AMC contracts in Delhi NCR, Noida, Bhopal, and Nagpur, our average on-site technician response is within 2 to 4 hours. We carry backup standby cameras, power adapters, and loaner NVRs to ensure your property is never left unrecorded during repairs.

Are your fire alarm and surveillance installations compliant with legal safety norms?

Yes. Digitech is an ISO 9001:2015 certified company. All our fire alarm designs strictly comply with National Building Code (NBC) guidelines, relevant IS standards, and local fire department audit requirements. We provide complete testing, commissioning, and handover documentation.

How do we get started with a free site survey?
